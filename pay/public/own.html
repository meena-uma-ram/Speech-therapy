Use TJS Pay in your clinic

The demo runs on the developer’s test PayPal account. A clinic never uses that: payments go to whichever PayPal account the copy is connected to. So each clinic runs its own copy, connected to its own PayPal account, with its data in its own database. It’s free and open source (AGPL).

What you need

  • A PayPal Business account for the clinic (this is where patients’ payments arrive).
  • A free Cloudflare account (it runs the app and keeps your data).
  • A free GitHub account (Cloudflare copies the code there for you).
  • Optional: an Anthropic API key for the AI assistants. Without it, TJS Pay uses its built-in rules.

Five steps

  1. Get your PayPal keys. At developer.paypal.com → Apps & Credentials → Sandbox → Create app (type Merchant). Tick Invoicing and Payouts. Copy the Client ID and Secret. Start with Sandbox (test money); you can switch to Live later.
  2. Deploy. Click the button below and sign in to Cloudflare and GitHub.
    Deploy to Cloudflare
  3. Enter your secrets when Cloudflare asks: PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID, PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET, an ADMIN_PASSCODE your staff will type to open TJS Pay (required), a long random LINK_SECRET, and optionally ANTHROPIC_API_KEY. Set PRACTICE_NAME to your clinic’s name. If you aren’t asked, add them afterwards in Cloudflare: your Worker → Settings → Variables and Secrets.
  4. Open your copy (Cloudflare shows its address) and enter the passcode. It starts empty: rename the sample departments and set their budgets and PayPal emails (Finance → Budgets and requests), check service prices (Billing office → Settings), then add your patients.
  5. Optional: in your PayPal app add a webhook to https://<your-copy>/api/paypal/webhook for “Invoicing invoice paid, updated, cancelled” and “Payouts”, and save its ID as PAYPAL_WEBHOOK_ID. Payments then show up at once instead of within the hour.

Your copy is safe by default

  • It needs the passcode before anyone sees patients or money.
  • Invoices go to your patients and payouts to your departments and suppliers. Nothing is redirected to a demo account.
  • Demo data can’t be loaded into it, and updates never erase your data.
  • Your keys are stored as Cloudflare secrets and never reach the browser.

Before real money

  • Try everything with Sandbox keys first. For real payments, create a Live app in PayPal, use its keys, and set PAYPAL_MODE to live.
  • Have the setup checked for your country: a data processing agreement with Cloudflare (and Anthropic if you use the AI), your privacy notice, and a code review. TJS Pay stores billing data only, never diagnoses or session notes.
