# Terms of Use, licence and warranty (5.3) – OUTLINE for the lawyer ⚖️

Two different documents, because they cover two different things:

| | Software licence | Terms of Use / Service Agreement |
|---|---|---|
| Covers | The code | Your hosted service (hosting, support, updates, SMS, the family portal) |
| Text | GNU AGPL v3 or later (LICENSE). Not changed | Your own contract with each clinic |
| Warranty | None ("as is", AGPL ss. 15–16) | Whatever you promise: uptime, support hours, backups, security measures |

You can't add conditions to the AGPL for the code beyond the section 7 terms already in NOTICE (the author credit, marking changed versions, and the name). You can sell services around it.

## Outline of the Service Agreement with a clinic

1. **Parties and definitions.** Clinic (customer, Data Fiduciary); [your legal entity] (provider, Data Processor).
2. **The service.** Hosted TJS: features, the region (India), support channels and hours.
3. **Clinic's responsibilities.** Lawful basis and consent; its own privacy notice; correct use; who gets accounts; leavers removed; patients' requests; clinical decisions are always the clinician's (TJS doesn't diagnose or recommend treatment).
4. **Accounts and security.** MFA required; no shared accounts; the clinic tells you at once about suspected misuse.
5. **Fees, invoices, taxes** (GST).
6. **Data protection.** The DPA ([04-dpa.md](04-dpa.md)) is part of this agreement.
7. **Availability and backups.** Target uptime [99.5%], backup frequency, restore time, planned maintenance notice.
8. **Intellectual property.** The software is AGPL; the clinic can get the source code of the version it uses. The clinic owns its data. Your trademark "TJS" is not licensed.
9. **Warranties and liability.** What you promise; disclaimers; a liability cap [12 months' fees]; exclusions that the law doesn't allow (check Indian Contract Act and Consumer Protection Act limits); no exclusion for your own breach of the DPA or data protection law where the law doesn't allow it.
10. **Term, termination, exit.** Export of all data in a documented format; deletion within [30] days after exit, with a deletion receipt; backup purge window.
11. **Changes to the service.** Notice period for material changes.
12. **Governing law and disputes.** [Indian law, courts of …] or arbitration [seat]. You are in Sweden: decide deliberately.

## Terms of Use for individual users (staff, students)

Short: acceptable use, confidentiality, no screenshots or exports of patient data outside the clinic's rules, device rules (screen lock, updates), reporting incidents, consequences of misuse.
