# Privacy notice (5.1) – DRAFT

👩 drafts, ⚖️ reviews. Two layers: the **short notice** patients see when they consent (already in the app, `js/consent.js`), and this **full notice** on the clinic's website and on paper at reception. Each clinic (the Data Fiduciary) publishes its own; TJS supplies the template.

**Versioning.** Every change gets a new version number. A change to a purpose, a recipient or the retention period is a *major* change (1.x → 2.0) and makes the app ask patients again. Wording fixes are minor (1.0 → 1.1). Keep every version, with its dates, for as long as consents given under it are kept.

**Languages.** English, Tamil, Hindi, Malayalam and Kannada (and any other language of the Eighth Schedule a patient asks for: DPDP Rules, Rule 3). Translations by a qualified translator, checked by a clinician; status "approved" only after that.

## [Clinic name] – how we use your personal information

Version [1.0] · in force from [date] · [languages available]

**Who we are.** [Clinic legal name, address]. We decide how your information is used (we are the "Data Fiduciary"). Contact for privacy questions and complaints: [name/role, email, phone, address].

**What we collect.** About the patient: name, date of birth, contact details, the person who brings them, the reason for referral, assessment results, therapy goals, session notes, progress reports, recordings only if you agree separately [delete if none]. About you (parent, guardian or carer): name, relationship, phone number, and your consent answers and signature.

**Why, and on what basis.**

| Purpose | Basis | Can you say no? |
|---|---|---|
| Assessment and therapy | Your consent to treatment | Yes, but we can't then provide therapy |
| Keeping records to provide safe, continuous care | Your consent; and records laws for [state/type of establishment] | You can withdraw; we may still have to keep records for [period] |
| Supervised student clinicians | Your consent | Yes; your care is not affected |
| Progress reports sent to you | Your consent | Yes |
| Appointment reminders by SMS / WhatsApp Business | Your consent | Yes |
| Pseudonymised use in teaching and coursework | Your consent | Yes |
| Medical emergency | Legitimate use (DPDP Act s. 7) | – |

**Who sees it.** Only the clinicians caring for the patient and their supervisors; students only with your consent. Our service providers process it for us under contract: [TJS / hosting provider, SMS provider, WhatsApp provider – see the list at …]. We don't sell information or use it for advertising.

**Where it is stored.** [On the clinic's own computer in [city]] / [On servers in Mumbai, India]. [If anything is processed outside India, say where and why.]

**How long.** [N] years after therapy ends, or longer only if a law requires it. Then it is erased, and backups are made unreadable.

**Children.** For a patient under 18, a parent or legal guardian gives consent. We still explain things to the child as far as they can understand.

**Adults who cannot decide for themselves.** A legal guardian or nominated representative decides, and we record the legal basis.

**Your rights.** See your information; correct it; withdraw any consent at any time (it stops from then on, and doesn't undo what was done before); ask for erasure; nominate someone to act for you if you die or become unable to; complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India. We reply within [30] days.

**Security.** Encryption, individual sign-in, access only for those who need it, an access log, backups. If there is a breach that affects you, we will tell you without delay.

**Changes.** We will tell you about important changes and ask again where needed.
